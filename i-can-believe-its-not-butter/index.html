I Can Believe It’s Not Butter

You know what?

I can believe that it’s not butter.

It doesn’t melt at room temperature.

It’s not appropriately salted.

It tastes of disappointment and an outdated belief that saturated fats and cholesterols must be avoided at all costs.

“The serving of colored oleomargarine or margarine at a public eating place as a substitute for table butter is prohibited unless it is ordered by the customer.”
— Wis. Stat. § 97.18(4)
“Any person who violates any provision of this section may be fined not less than $100 nor more than $500 or imprisoned not more than 3 months or both”
— Wis. Stat. § 97.18(6)
I will never believe that it is butter.
